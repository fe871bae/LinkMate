Write a note today, ask a question later

1 Capture

A note in your own words, or a pasted email

2 Review

AI proposes a profile, you correct and confirm

3 Ask

Describe a need in natural language

4 Act

Contacts with reason and source note, or “no suitable contact”

No forms, no tagging, and every recommendation is traceable.
