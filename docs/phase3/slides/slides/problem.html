Help exists in your network, but you can’t find it

The capture moment

You meet someone. What they do and offer ends up nowhere, or as a loose note.

The need moment

Weeks later you have a problem, and can’t remember who could help.

Contact books store a name and a company, not what a person can do. Acquaintances are a key source of new information and jobs (Granovetter 1973, Rajkumar et al. 2022), and their context is lost first.

Knowledge sits in phone notes, emails, chats and memory. Help that exists in your network goes unused.
